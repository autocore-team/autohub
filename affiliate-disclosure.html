Affiliate Disclosure

This page explains how AutoHub may use partner links while keeping the tools and recommendations useful, transparent and independent.

Short version

Last updated: July 18, 2026.

Some AutoHub pages may include affiliate links in the future. If you click such a link and buy a product or service, AutoHub may receive a commission at no extra cost to you.

Why affiliate links may be used

AutoHub is being built as a free automotive tool portal. Partner income can help pay for hosting, data work, testing, translations and future improvements.

Where links may appear

  • Diagnostic adapters, OBD2 tools and software access.
  • Vehicle parts, wheels, tires and fitment-related products.
  • Motor oil, fluids and maintenance items.
  • Vehicle history, VIN-related services or technical data providers.

Editorial independence

Affiliate relationships should not decide whether a tool, product or service is described as suitable. AutoHub should focus on compatibility, official sources, practical usefulness and user safety.

Compatibility still matters

Before buying anything, always check your vehicle year, engine code, VIN details, wheel fitment, oil approval, diagnostic protocol, cable type and local rules where relevant.

Diagnostic software note

AutoHub should not promote pirated software, cracks, activators or unsafe downloads. Official, licensed or clearly legitimate sources should be preferred.

Current status

AutoHub may not yet contain active affiliate links. This disclosure is added early so the project is ready before monetized links are introduced.

Questions or cooperation

Use the contact page to report unclear links, suggest corrections or discuss cooperation related to automotive tools, data, parts or services.
