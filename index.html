How to read tire markings

Example on tire sidewall 205/55 R16 91V XL DOT 2423

The main size is only part of the tire label. Load index, speed rating, reinforced construction and production date also matter when choosing tires.

Marking Meaning Example
205 Tire section width in millimeters. 205 mm
55 Sidewall height as a percentage of the width. 55% of 205 mm
R Radial tire construction. R = radial
16 Rim diameter in inches. 16 inch wheel
91 Load index. It must meet or exceed the vehicle requirement. 91 = up to 615 kg per tire
V Speed rating. It must be suitable for the car and local rules. V = up to 240 km/h
XL Reinforced tire with higher load capacity at the correct pressure. XL / Extra Load
DOT 2423 Production date: week and year. Here it means week 24 of 2023. 24 / 2023
M+S / 3PMSF Season and winter performance markings. 3PMSF is the snowflake mountain symbol. All-season or winter tire
Outside / Rotation Mounting direction or outside sidewall marking for asymmetric or directional tires. Install as marked

Diameter tolerance

A tire diameter change within about 3% is commonly treated as a practical limit, but always verify fitment for your vehicle.

Offset and clearance

Rim width and ET affect how close the wheel sits to the suspension and how far it moves toward the fender.

Speedometer

A smaller tire makes the actual road speed lower than the displayed speed. A larger tire does the opposite.

Always verify wheel fitment with your vehicle manual or a professional before buying. Brake clearance, suspension modifications and local regulations may affect compatibility.
