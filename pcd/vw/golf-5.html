Variants and exclusions

  • Golf V hatchback only. Golf Plus, Golf Variant and Jetta are excluded from this verified record.
  • GTI begins in the Volkswagen history at 2004; R32 is recorded from September 2005. Their historical factory wheel combinations are not imported in this batch.
  • The archived Volkswagen Wheels and Tyres Guide used during research returned HTTP 403 on re-check. Its historical combinations therefore remain unasserted rather than being guessed.
  • No generic ET45–51 range is used for the verified Golf V record.
