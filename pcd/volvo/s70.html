Scope and restrictions

  • European S70 sedan, Type L; P80 is retained as a search alias.
  • Check FWD/AWD, diesel applicability, steering limiter and the installed 280/302/320 mm brake package.
  • All listed wheel offsets are exactly ET43. The former ET43–49 generalisation has been removed.
