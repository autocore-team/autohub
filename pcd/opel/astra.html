Scope and safety warning

  • Generation/body envelope includes 5-door hatchback, GTC 3-door, TwinTop, Caravan/Estate, Sedan and Astravan, but exact periods differ.
  • Do not select 4x100 or 5x110 from engine, power output or body style alone.
  • Check the physical bolt count, then confirm with the CoC, type approval or VIN-specific documentation.
  • ET45, 215/50R17 and an undocumented 19-inch VXR width/ET are intentionally excluded.
