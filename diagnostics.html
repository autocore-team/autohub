Diagnostic software for cars

OBD2, ELM327, KKL, VIDA/DICE, VAG, Volvo, BMW, Opel, Toyota and other diagnostic tools in one practical reference. Use this page to choose the right software path before you buy a cable, install drivers or connect to a car.

  • GoalFind legal diagnostic software, official sources and compatible adapter types.
  • ImportantCompatibility depends on car year, diagnostic protocol, adapter quality and operating system.
  • SafetyReading codes is usually low risk. Coding, flashing and adaptations require extra care.

Compatibility checklist

Check before downloading

  • Car make, model and production year.
  • OBD2, CAN, K-Line, KKL, J2534, ENET, DICE or other protocol.
  • USB, Bluetooth or Wi-Fi cable type.
  • Windows version and driver compatibility.
  • Whether the tool supports your exact control module.
Before downloading diagnostic software

Before downloading diagnostic software, check your car year, diagnostic protocol, cable type, Windows version and driver compatibility.
