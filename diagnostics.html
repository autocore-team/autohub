Legal sources only

Use official websites, licensed software, app stores and legitimate vendor pages. Avoid pirated builds, cracks and activators.

Adapter matters

A bad adapter can give connection errors, missing modules or unstable coding sessions. Match the adapter to the software and vehicle.

Coding and flashing risk

Reading codes is usually low risk. Coding, adaptations, immobilizer work and ECU flashing can damage modules if done incorrectly.
