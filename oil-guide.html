Compatibility result

Manufacturer approval first

A 5W-30 oil without the required approval may be wrong. Match the OEM approval before comparing brands.

Viscosity is not compatibility

SAE describes cold and hot flow behavior. It does not replace API, ACEA, ILSAC or manufacturer requirements.

DPF and GPF matter

Engines with particulate filters may require low-ash oil. The wrong oil can shorten filter life.

How to read an oil specification

  1. SAE viscosity: examples include 0W-20, 5W-30 and 5W-40.
  2. Industry class: API, ACEA or ILSAC defines performance requirements.
  3. Manufacturer approval: examples include BMW Longlife-01, VW 504 00 or MB 229.5.
  4. Exact vehicle: engine code, model year, market and emissions equipment can change the answer.
