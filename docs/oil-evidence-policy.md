# Oil evidence policy: source first, field-specific scope

This policy guides evidence review alongside [the implemented field-evidence
rules](oil-field-evidence.md). It does not retrospectively confirm records,
change statuses, or authorize bypassing the current validator. Implementation
limitations must be recorded separately and reviewed before publication.

1. **Market identifies applicability, not an oil formula.** Use market evidence
   to identify the vehicle, installed engine and applicable document. A different
   market alone does not establish different oil requirements. Conversely, an
   unchanged engine name does not prove a document applies to another vehicle.

2. **An engine code does not establish universal installation equivalence.**
   Separate claims where evidence establishes a relevant difference in engine
   version, sump, filter, oil cooler, emissions hardware, manufacturer requirement
   or other installation/use condition. Do not automatically demand trim,
   transmission, drivetrain or hardware attributes when they are not conditions
   of the particular field's documented applicability.

3. **Assess requirements and quantity independently.** A common oil specification
   may cover multiple applications while quantity has a narrower scope. For a
   quantity, retain filter inclusion, approximate/reference character, fill type
   and checking procedure. Evaluate maintenance context; neither a missing magic
   phrase nor the words "including filter" alone settle drain/refill versus dry
   capacity. Unresolved quantity stays unknown without invalidating an otherwise
   confirmed oil-selection path. Unknown/conflicting values are not recommendations.

4. **Preserve the manufacturer's choice and conditions.** Where a factory
   temperature chart exists, retain the applicable SAE choices and temperature/use
   conditions. Keep required oil quality, preferred SAE, temporary substitutes,
   mandatory return instructions and special conditions distinct. Do not choose
   one viscosity for the user, flatten AND/OR, or turn a temporary substitute into
   an unconditional alternative. Without a published chart, do not invent
   temperature ranges or additional SAE grades. A recommended product is not
   automatically a compulsory brand or an independently established OEM approval.

5. **State provenance accurately.** Distinguish manufacturer-hosted official
   publication, third-party copy attributed to a manufacturer, and independent
   reference material. Record what establishes provenance and what remains
   uncertain; host familiarity does not authenticate every value. Keep
   reference-only information separate from recommendation values and eligibility.

6. **Work from documents to demonstrated coverage.** Preserve source identity,
   edition/update, exact URL, locators, excerpts, application/field scope,
   limitations and decisions. Reuse established facts; hashes establish cache
   integrity, not applicability by themselves. Revisit a gap when a new source or
   concrete discriminator can close it. Do not create year/trim duplicates to
   inflate counts or carry values between engine codes without evidence.

7. **Separate documentary gaps from implementation gaps.** A parser rejection,
   unsupported source host, or narrow scope representation does not prove physical
   inapplicability or unreliable content. Report the exact condition and source
   wording, distinguishing conflict, insufficient evidence and implementation
   restriction. Keep original excerpts; do not rewrite evidence to pass a check,
   invent GLOBAL scope, relax verified/corroborated requirements or promote status.

Pilot applying these principles: [Volvo XC40 2021](oil-volvo-xc40-source-pilot.md).
