Privacy Policy

This page explains how AutoHub handles information on this static automotive website.

Short version

Last updated: July 18, 2026.

AutoHub is currently a static GitHub Pages website. It does not require an account, does not ask for payment details and does not intentionally store VIN numbers or vehicle search history.

Information that may be processed

  • Basic technical access data may be processed by the hosting provider when a page is loaded.
  • Calculator values can appear in the page URL when you copy or share a calculation link.
  • The selected language may be stored in the page URL or in your browser so the site can reopen in the same language.
  • If you contact AutoHub by email, your email address and message content are received so the request can be answered.

Why this information is used

  • To load the website and keep the tools working.
  • To show the selected language.
  • To let users share calculator results with URL parameters.
  • To respond to corrections, broken-link reports and cooperation messages.

Cookies and local storage

AutoHub may use browser storage only for simple preferences such as language. You can clear this data in your browser settings. Advertising or analytics tools may be added later only with an updated privacy notice.

External links

AutoHub links to external websites such as official diagnostic software pages, service portals and partner resources. Those websites have their own privacy policies and are not controlled by AutoHub.

VIN and vehicle data

AutoHub does not currently store VIN numbers. If a VIN-related tool is added later, it should be designed to avoid storing VINs unless a clear reason and privacy notice are provided.

Your choices

  • You can clear browser storage and URL parameters at any time.
  • You can avoid sending personal data by email unless it is necessary for your request.
  • You can contact AutoHub to ask about correction or deletion of messages you sent.

Privacy contact

For privacy questions, corrections or deletion requests, contact AutoHub by email.
